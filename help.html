Combat Economy (Shared Pool)

Shared Shield + Energy

Core system

Firing and active abilities consume both energy and a portion of shield.

Impact: the more you fire, the lower your defense goes. Actions are blocked at zero shield.

Different Drain Factors

Balancing

Primary has the lowest drain, secondary/utility are higher, dash is mid.

Impact: weapon identity stays clear, with explicit risk/reward tradeoff.

Mission Types

Mission order cycles as survive -> ufo_hunt -> asteroid_storm -> mini_boss. In campaign mode, mini_boss in the final sector becomes FINAL BOSS (distinct moveset and phases), and clearing it ends the run with VICTORY.

SURVIVE

Hold timer + cleanup

Survive the time limit. After timer expires, objective switches to clearing remaining threats.

Impact: tempo management, space control, and stable resource economy.

UFO HUNT

Kill quota

Complete UFO kill quota. Spawn tempo and parallel pressure increase by sector.

Impact: target-priority execution and reading enemy roles (support/sniper/kamikaze).

ASTEROID STORM

Break quota

Break target number of asteroids. After quota, cleanup of remaining threats still applies.

Impact: arena overflow risk; chain/AOE loadouts are efficient.

MINI BOSS

Phase + weakpoint

Boss fight with phases, weakpoint windows, and phase events. Kill boss, then finish cleanup.

Impact: burst-window discipline vs survival, with correct utility/dash timing.

Endless mode unlocks after your first campaign clear. On start screen, toggle campaign/endless with E.

Biomes

Each sector rolls a biome. Biome defines arena atmosphere and may add specific hazard zones.

Belt Fringe

Biome

Standard asteroid belt edge. Neutral zone without special biome hazard.

Impact: cleanest baseline for combat and build comparison.

Wreck Graveyard

Biome

Wreck zone filled with debris. Typically adds Debris Field hazard.

Impact: reduced mobility, higher collision risk, and chip damage.

Refinery Complex

Biome

Industrial refinery zone with thermal vents. Typically adds Plasma Vent hazard.

Impact: rapid heat gain and high positioning pressure.

Ion Field

Biome

Charged ionized area. Neutral by itself, often paired with global mission modifiers.

Impact: visual/atmospheric pressure; combined with Ion Storm it increases resource stress.

Pilot RPG (MVP)

XP and Level

Pilot progression

You gain XP from score and mission completion. Level-ups grant attribute points and every two levels also a skill point.

Impact: long-term progression between sectors, not only immediate loot spikes.

Attributes

Reflex / Systems / Grit / Instinct

Reflex improves handling and cadence, Systems improves regen/cooldowns, Grit improves toughness, Instinct improves crit and damage economy.

Impact: clear build identity from early game onward.

Perk unlock

Hangar controls

In hangar, use Left/Right for sections, Up/Down for selection, and Space for action. Immediate Actions includes Sell selected and Salvage selected without legacy keys. Legacy keys T/Y/U/I/O/K and 9/0 remain fallback.

Impact: controlled specialization without chaotic random buffing.

Pilot Identity and Ship Frames

In start/game over/victory overlays, choose your identity with Up/Down for pilot and A/D for ship frame. Identity adds small stat flavor bonuses.

Pilot Roster (4)

Identity layer

Needle Voss (precision cooldown+crit), Graveline Kade (shield sustain + collision tolerance), Rook-86 (heavy hull/shield), Blink Serrat (mobility burst).

Impact: clear playstyle fantasy without replacing loadout/build decisions.

Ship Frames (4)

Identity layer

Viper MK-II (speed), Bastion Frame (durability), Revenant Frame (damage pressure), Helix Frame (systems/regen).

Impact: players can self-identify with a frame archetype while keeping balancing in a safe 5-10% range.

Asteroids

Normal Asteroid

Object

Classic asteroid. Splits into smaller parts on hit.

Impact: neutral threat, good source of score/loot.

Magnetic Asteroid

Object

Pulls the ship in close radius and distorts trajectory.

Impact: weaker maneuvering and higher collision risk.

Volatile Asteroid

Object

On destruction, triggers a blast that can chain additional hits.

Impact: AOE hazard, but also a tactical weapon against packs.

UFO Types

Hunter

Enemy

Close- to mid-range pressure.

Impact: stable DPS threat.

Sniper

Enemy

Keeps distance and fires precise shots.

Impact: punishes static positioning.

Swarm

Enemy

Fast orbit and circular pressure.

Impact: can overwhelm weak aim tracking.

Kamikaze

Enemy

Attempts collision and detonates on contact.

Impact: melee burst damage.

Support

Enemy

Heals nearby UFO units.

Impact: extends fights, high focus-target priority.

Mine Layer

Enemy

Deploys mines and controls space.

Impact: zoning pressure, forces repositioning.

Hazard Zones

Debris Field

Biome Hazard (Wreck Graveyard)

Debris sphere. Slows the ship inside and applies periodic damage.

Impact: mobility loss + chip damage.

Plasma Vent

Biome Hazard (Refinery Complex)

Pulsing thermal zone. Increases heat and applies thermal tick damage.

Impact: rapid overheat and action limitations.

Gravity Anomaly

Mission Modifier

Pulls ship and asteroids toward anomaly center. Pull is softened in the core to avoid hard-lock behavior.

Impact: trajectory distortion and collision setups; escape by holding thrust (ArrowUp) or boost (Shift) outward from the core.

Ion Storm

Mission Modifier

Reduces shield regen and slowly drains shield.

Impact: weaker sustain and higher dodge pressure.

Dev Profiling Controls

For runtime performance diagnostics during manual QA, use B to toggle the perf overlay and N to dump a profiling snapshot to the browser console.
